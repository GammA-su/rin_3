Gemini is a generative artificial intelligence chatbot developed by Google AI. Based on the large language model (LLM) of the same name, it was launched in February 2024. Its predecessor, Bard, was launched in March 2023 in response to the rise of OpenAI's ChatGPT agent and was based on the LaMDA and PaLM LLMs.
