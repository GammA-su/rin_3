Neural systems use the same underlying computational substrate to carry out
analog filtering and signal processing operations, as well as discrete symbol
manipulation and digital computation. Inspired by the computational principles
of canonical cortical microcircuits, we propose a framework for using recurrent
spiking neural networks to seamlessly and robustly switch between analog signal
processing and categorical and discrete computation. We provide theoretical
analysis and practical neural network design tools to formally determine the
conditions for inducing this switch. We demonstrate the robustness of this
framework experimentally with hardware soft Winner-Take-All and mixed-feedback
recurrent spiking neural networks, implemented by appropriately configuring the
analog neuron and synapse circuits of a mixed-signal neuromorphic processor
chip.
