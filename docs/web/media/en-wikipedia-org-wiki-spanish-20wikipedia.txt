The Spanish Wikipedia is the Spanish-language edition of Wikipedia, a free online encyclopedia. It has 2,059,416 articles. Started in May 2001, it reached 100,000 articles on 8 March 2006, and 1,000,000 articles on 16 May 2013. It is the 8th-largest Wikipedia as measured by the number of articles and has the 4th-most edits. It also ranks 32nd in terms of article depth among Wikipedias.
